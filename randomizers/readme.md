These are files to be used for people's randomizer playthroughs. This will likely mostly just be Pokémon games.

Warp Randomizer:
These are randomizers created by https://warprandomizer.com/. Entering any warp (like a door or a cave) will instead take you somewhere random to create a huge maze. This does not change the starting towns, but anything after that is fair game. For example, the first Pokémon Center in Pokémon Emerald in Oldale Town may instead take you to Team Aqua's hideout, or the Pokémon Center in that town may take you to Fortree City.
